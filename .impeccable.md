# Lightly — Design Context

## Users

Online Texas Hold'em players, 25–45, crypto-native. They're at the table late at night in a dim room, eyes tired, wanting focus — not spectacle. They've played PokerStars, GGPoker, Stake. They know what a felt looks like; they don't need reminders. They care about:

- fast, clear action prompts with zero ambiguity
- trustworthy-feeling numbers (tabular, no jitter)
- visible cryptographic receipts (the VRF commit, the settlement sig) without those being in their face
- a product that looks private and earned, not algorithmic

Context: 90% desktop in landscape, 10% mobile vertical (sideways hold is uncomfortable so mobile is an afterthought, not a priority).

## Brand Personality

Three words: **quiet, exacting, after-hours.**

- *Quiet* — no neon, no "web3 glow", no marketing-deck gradients. A pro tool.
- *Exacting* — numbers are tabular; the pot is always perfectly aligned; animations decelerate, never overshoot.
- *After-hours* — dark surfaces, warm muted accents, the feel of a private card room at 11pm, not a casino floor.

Emotional goal: when a player opens Lightly they feel **focused** (this is serious) and **held** (the chain is there, quietly, doing its job).

## Aesthetic Direction

- **Theme**: Dark. Derived from the real viewing context (late night, dim room, headphones on).
- **Felt color**: Deep green, desaturated. Evocative of real felt — not a neon green, not a Stripe-style teal.
- **Brand hue**: Green (`oklch(0.42 0.05 150)` as the anchor). Neutrals tint slightly toward green so surfaces feel cohesive.
- **Accent**: Warm amber/brass (`oklch(0.76 0.12 75)`), used sparingly — call-to-act ring, winning chip glow, the brand mark. Absolutely no purple, no cyan, no magenta.
- **Reference marks**: a private poker room, mechanical wristwatch dial, linotype newsprint, vintage book typography. Not: Vegas, Stake casino UI, crypto exchanges.
- **Anti-references**: glowing purple gradients, AI-generated "cyberpunk"/"synthwave" palettes, drop shadows with colored glow, glassmorphism blur as decoration.

## Typography

- **Display** (brand wordmark, scorecards, showdown labels): **Bricolage Grotesque** — variable, distinctive, not yet overused.
- **Body / UI text**: **Geist Sans** — clean geometric sans, tabular-nums naturally available, not in impeccable's ban list.
- **Mono / numerics** (pot values, stacks, chip pills, tx sigs): **Geist Mono** — for serious tabular alignment.
- **Weight hierarchy**: 400 body, 500 labels, 600 numeric emphasis, 700 display only for brand.
- Ban list (never): Inter, Fraunces, Newsreader, IBM Plex, DM Sans, Playfair, Syne, Instrument Serif.

## Design Principles

1. **Tabular or GTFO.** Every number uses tabular-nums. No number should ever jitter when it updates.
2. **One accent, one meaning.** Amber/brass means "your turn or your win." It appears nowhere else. No purple accents; no decorative colored chrome.
3. **Motion decelerates, never bounces.** `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.2, 1)` everywhere. No spring with overshoot, no elastic, no pulse at rest.
4. **Rhythm, not uniformity.** Tight groups (8/12) inside generous groups (32/48/64). The table breathes; the sidebar is dense.
5. **The chain is there, not on stage.** Settlement tx sigs live in a drawer; commit hashes are a small mono line at the felt corner; the UI primary speaks in dollars ($12.30), not micro-USDC or lamports.
